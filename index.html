EduOasis

Upload your notes and they become a game world on your own island. Like memories, islands dry up as a forgetting-curve model predicts you're forgetting, so you come back and play to keep them alive. Gemini builds the worlds, Pip the AI companion answers from your notes, and a World Market runs on MOE, a real Token-2022 token on Solana devnet.

  • Next.js
  • React
  • Phaser
  • Gemini
  • Solana

My part: worked across the whole stack: front end, back end and integrations.

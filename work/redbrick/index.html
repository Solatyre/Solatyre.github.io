Questions investors ask us

Can't see your question? Ask on the investor list form and we'll reply by email.

How much is your sourcing fee?

Between £2,500 and £5,000 depending on the deal, shown on every listing. You pay £500 to reserve and the rest on completion.

Do you buy the properties yourselves?

No. We find and negotiate the deal, then you buy directly from the vendor. That keeps your stamp duty and legal costs lower.

Can I invest from outside the UK?

Yes. About a third of our investors live abroad. We do video viewings and work with brokers who lend to overseas buyers.

What checks do you do on me?

As an AML-supervised business we verify your identity and where your funds come from before you reserve a deal.

Get new deals before they're listed

Tell us your budget and strategy. We only send deals that match, usually two or three a month.

Common questions

Can clients pay without creating an account?

Yes. Every invoice has a payment link. Clients pay by card, Apple Pay or bank transfer without signing up.

Will reminders annoy my clients?

You choose the timing and the wording, and you can pause reminders for any client. They stop as soon as an invoice is paid.

Do I need to be VAT registered?

No. Turn VAT off and Tallyfold leaves it off every invoice. Turn it on when you register and add your VAT number once.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You keep read-only access to every invoice you've sent, and can export them all as PDFs.
